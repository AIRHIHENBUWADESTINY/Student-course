language — no technical knowledge required. You will learn how to create an
account, find and register courses, submit your registration, read your
lessons, and track your progress.

## 1. What is Deeplearn?

Deeplearn is a student app that brings your semester together in one place. With
it you can:

- Browse the **course catalogue** and see course details, fees and credit units.
- **Register** for the courses you want to take this semester.
- Follow **lessons** for your courses.
- Track your **learning progress**.
- Get help from an **AI assistant** when choosing courses.

The app works in any modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

## 2. Creating your account

1. Open the Deeplearn website.
2. On the welcome screen, select **Get started** (or **Sign in** if you already
   have an account).
3. Choose the **Create account** option.
4. Enter your name, email address and a password.
5. Submit the form. You are signed in automatically and taken to your dashboard.

> If your school has **email confirmation** switched on, you may be asked to
> confirm your email address before you can sign in. Check your inbox and spam
> folder for the confirmation message.

## 3. Signing in and out

- **To sign in:** select **Sign in**, enter your email and password, then
  submit.
- **To sign out:** open the account menu in the top corner of the screen and
  choose **Sign out**.

Keep your password private. If you forget it, ask your school administrator to
help you reset it.

## 4. Your dashboard

The dashboard is your home screen. It shows:

- A welcome message and your **academic details** (faculty, department and
  level).
- Summary cards with your **registered courses**, **total credit units** and
  **total course fee**.
- A **Registered courses** list with the fee for each course.
- Buttons to **Add course** or open the catalogue.

## 5. Finding courses

1. Open the **Course Catalogue** from the menu or the dashboard.
2. Use the filters to narrow the list by **faculty**, **department** or
   **level**.
3. Select a course to see its description, credit units and fee.
4. Read carefully before choosing — some courses belong to specific
   departments or levels.

## 6. Registering for courses

There are two easy ways to add a course:

**From the catalogue**

1. Open a course and choose **Add to registration** (or the equivalent button).
2. The course is added to your current registration.

**From the dashboard**

1. Select **Add course**.
2. Pick a **category**, then a **course title**.
3. Confirm the credit units and fee, then submit.
4. The course appears in your **Registered courses** list.

You can remove a course you have not submitted yet by selecting the **trash
icon** next to it.

## 7. Understanding fees and credit units

- **Credit units** measure how much of your study load a course represents.
  Deeplearn lets you register **as many credit units as you need** — there is no
  limit per semester.
- **Course fee** is the price of each course. Your **total course fee** is the
  sum of all the courses in your registration.

Open **My Registered Courses** to see your totals. The **Course fee breakdown**
panel always shows:

- The fee for your **previous submitted course list**.
- The fee for any **new courses** you have added since your last submission,
  calculated separately and highlighted.

## 8. Submitting your registration

When your course list is complete:

1. Open **My Registered Courses**.
2. Review the courses, credit units and fees.
3. Select **Submit registration** and confirm.

After you submit, a **Registration submitted** badge appears.

> **Important:** once submitted, you cannot change or remove courses online.
> Contact your school administrator if you need to make a change.

## 9. Adding courses after you have submitted

You can keep building your registration after you submit:

1. Add more courses from the catalogue or dashboard as usual.
2. The new courses are tagged **NEW** so you can tell them apart from your
   previously submitted courses.
3. On your **Registered Courses** page, courses are grouped into
   **New since last submission** and **Previously submitted**, each with its own
   subtotal.
4. Select **Submit registration** again to submit just the new courses. Your
   earlier courses are not affected.

## 10. Reading lessons

1. Open **Lessons** from the menu.
2. Select a course, then choose a lesson to read it.
3. Lesson content opens in a clean reading view. Use the browser back button to
   return to the lesson list.

> Some sample courses do not have lesson text yet. Lessons appear as they are
> added by your school.

## 11. Tracking your progress

- Open **Progress** to see how far you have come in each course.
- Lessons you open and complete are marked automatically.
- Use the progress bars and percentages to see your overall completion.
- An administrator can reset progress if needed.

## 12. Course materials

Open **Materials** to reach the reading material attached to your registered
courses. Materials are organised by course so everything for a subject is in
one place.

## 13. Using the AI assistant

The AI assistant is a chat helper that can:

- Suggest catalogue courses that match your interests.
- Add or remove courses for you when you ask in plain language.
- Summarise your current registration, credit units and fees.

Open the chat from your dashboard, type a request such as *"add a programming
course"*, and follow the assistant's reply.

## 14. Your profile and settings

- **Profile:** keep your name and academic details up to date.
- **Settings:** adjust your application preferences.
- Review your details regularly so your registration stays accurate.

## 15. Questions and troubleshooting

**I can't sign in.**
Check your email and password, and make sure you confirmed your email address
if your school requires it. Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

**My course list is empty.**
Open the **Course Catalogue**, add courses, then return to **My Registered
Courses**.

**I can't remove a course.**
If you have already submitted your registration, courses are locked and cannot
be changed online. Ask your administrator for help.

**I can't find lessons for a course.**
Lesson text may not have been added for that course yet. Check **Materials**
instead, or try another course.

**The total fee looks wrong.**
Confirm that you have reviewed every course and refresh the page. Compare the
**Previous course list** and **New since last submission** figures in the
**Course fee breakdown** panel.
