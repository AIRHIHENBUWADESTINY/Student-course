# Robotics and Intelligent Systems

_Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

This course explores machines and software that sense their environments, make decisions, and perform actions. Students study sensors, control algorithms, navigation, perception, planning, and basic autonomous behaviour. Practical projects may use simulations or simple robotic platforms, helping learners understand how software, hardware, and intelligent decision-making combine to perform tasks.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up robotics and intelligent systems.
- Describe machines and how it is used.
- Describe software that sense their environments and how it is used.
- Describe make decisions and how it is used.
- Describe perform actions and how it is used.
- Describe sensors and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning explore techniques that enable computers to identify patterns, make predictions, understand information, and generate outputs. Students learn data preparation, model development, evaluation, responsible use, and the limitations of intelligent systems in practical applications.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about robotics and intelligent systems. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
