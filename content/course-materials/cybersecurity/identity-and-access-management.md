# Identity and Access Management

_Cybersecurity · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Identity and Access Management is part of the Cybersecurity area. Identity and Access Management examines how organizations control who can access systems and information. This self-study guide takes you from the core vocabulary through to a small practical project so you can learn at your own pace.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up identity and access management.
- Describe information and how it is used.
- Describe authentication and how it is used.
- Describe authorization and how it is used.
- Describe multifactor authentication and how it is used.
- Describe role-based access control and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from unauthorized access, misuse, and digital threats. Students learn security principles, risk assessment, defensive technologies, incident response, and ethical practices for maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital systems.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about identity and access management. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
