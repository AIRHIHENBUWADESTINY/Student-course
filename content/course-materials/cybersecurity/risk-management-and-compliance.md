# Risk Management and Compliance

_Cybersecurity · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

This course introduces methods for identifying, assessing, and managing information security risks. Students examine risk registers, security policies, control assessments, privacy requirements, and compliance frameworks. They learn to evaluate potential business impacts, recommend appropriate safeguards, and document decisions. The course connects technical security measures with organizational responsibilities and governance.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up risk management and compliance.
- Describe risk registers and how it is used.
- Describe security policies and how it is used.
- Describe control assessments and how it is used.
- Describe privacy requirements and how it is used.
- Describe compliance frameworks and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from unauthorized access, misuse, and digital threats. Students learn security principles, risk assessment, defensive technologies, incident response, and ethical practices for maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital systems.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about risk management and compliance. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
