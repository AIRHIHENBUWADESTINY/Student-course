# Design Systems

_UI/UX and Digital Product Design · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Design Systems examines reusable visual rules and components that help teams maintain consistent interfaces. Students explore component libraries, design tokens, typography, colour standards, documentation, and accessibility guidelines. Practical projects create a small design system and apply it across multiple screens, improving collaboration, consistency, efficiency, and the maintainability of digital product interfaces.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up design systems.
- Describe reusable visual rules and how it is used.
- Describe component libraries and how it is used.
- Describe design tokens and how it is used.
- Describe typography and how it is used.
- Describe colour standards and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

UI/UX and Digital Product Design focuses on creating digital products that are visually clear, accessible, and easy to use. Students learn user research, interface design, prototyping, usability evaluation, interaction principles, and design systems to improve how people interact with software.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about design systems. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
