# Wireframing and Prototyping

_UI/UX and Digital Product Design · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Wireframing and Prototyping teaches students how to represent and test product ideas before full development. Learners create low-fidelity layouts, interactive prototypes, navigation flows, and design specifications. Practical exercises use design tools to communicate requirements and gather feedback, reducing misunderstandings and allowing teams to identify usability problems early in the product lifecycle.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up wireframing and prototyping.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

UI/UX and Digital Product Design focuses on creating digital products that are visually clear, accessible, and easy to use. Students learn user research, interface design, prototyping, usability evaluation, interaction principles, and design systems to improve how people interact with software.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about wireframing and prototyping. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
