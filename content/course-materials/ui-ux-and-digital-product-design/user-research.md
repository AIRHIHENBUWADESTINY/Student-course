# User Research

_UI/UX and Digital Product Design · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

User Research introduces methods for understanding the people who will use a digital product. Students study interviews, surveys, observation, usability sessions, and research synthesis. Practical projects gather and interpret feedback to identify user needs and barriers, while emphasizing informed participation, privacy, inclusive research practices, and evidence-based design decisions.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up user research.
- Describe interviews and how it is used.
- Describe surveys and how it is used.
- Describe observation and how it is used.
- Describe usability sessions and how it is used.
- Describe research synthesis and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

UI/UX and Digital Product Design focuses on creating digital products that are visually clear, accessible, and easy to use. Students learn user research, interface design, prototyping, usability evaluation, interaction principles, and design systems to improve how people interact with software.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about user research. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
