# Interaction Design

_UI/UX and Digital Product Design · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Interaction Design is part of the UI/UX and Digital Product Design area. Interaction Design focuses on how users and digital systems respond to one another. This self-study guide takes you from the core vocabulary through to a small practical project so you can learn at your own pace.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up interaction design.
- Describe navigation patterns and how it is used.
- Describe feedback and how it is used.
- Describe transitions and how it is used.
- Describe states and how it is used.
- Describe error prevention and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

UI/UX and Digital Product Design focuses on creating digital products that are visually clear, accessible, and easy to use. Students learn user research, interface design, prototyping, usability evaluation, interaction principles, and design systems to improve how people interact with software.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about interaction design. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
