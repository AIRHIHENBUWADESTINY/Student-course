# 3D Modelling

_Digital Media and Creative Technology · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

3D Modelling is part of the Digital Media and Creative Technology area. 3D Modelling teaches students how to create three-dimensional digital objects and environments. This self-study guide takes you from the core vocabulary through to a small practical project so you can learn at your own pace.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up 3d modelling.
- Describe geometry and how it is used.
- Describe meshes and how it is used.
- Describe materials and how it is used.
- Describe textures and how it is used.
- Describe lighting and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Digital Media and Creative Technology combines artistic communication with digital tools used to create visual, animated, interactive, and audio content. Students learn design principles, media production, modelling, editing, and immersive technologies for education, entertainment, marketing, and digital communication.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about 3d modelling. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
