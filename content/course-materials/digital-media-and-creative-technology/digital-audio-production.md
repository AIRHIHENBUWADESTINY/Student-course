# Digital Audio Production

_Digital Media and Creative Technology · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Digital Audio Production teaches students how to record, edit, arrange, and mix audio using digital tools. Topics include sound capture, waveforms, audio tracks, effects, mixing, mastering, and export formats. Practical projects produce podcasts, voice recordings, or short audio pieces, emphasizing sound clarity, balanced levels, file organization, and responsible use of recorded material.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up digital audio production.
- Describe sound capture and how it is used.
- Describe waveforms and how it is used.
- Describe audio tracks and how it is used.
- Describe effects and how it is used.
- Describe mixing and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Digital Media and Creative Technology combines artistic communication with digital tools used to create visual, animated, interactive, and audio content. Students learn design principles, media production, modelling, editing, and immersive technologies for education, entertainment, marketing, and digital communication.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about digital audio production. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
