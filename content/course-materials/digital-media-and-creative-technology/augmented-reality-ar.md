# Augmented Reality (AR)

_Digital Media and Creative Technology · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Augmented Reality teaches students how digital content can be placed within views of the physical world. Topics include tracking, spatial mapping, interactive overlays, mobile AR frameworks, and user experience. Practical activities develop simple AR demonstrations, helping learners connect digital objects with real environments while addressing usability, device compatibility, privacy, and safe interaction.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up augmented reality (ar).
- Describe tracking and how it is used.
- Describe spatial mapping and how it is used.
- Describe interactive overlays and how it is used.
- Describe mobile AR frameworks and how it is used.
- Describe user experience and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Digital Media and Creative Technology combines artistic communication with digital tools used to create visual, animated, interactive, and audio content. Students learn design principles, media production, modelling, editing, and immersive technologies for education, entertainment, marketing, and digital communication.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about augmented reality (ar). Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
