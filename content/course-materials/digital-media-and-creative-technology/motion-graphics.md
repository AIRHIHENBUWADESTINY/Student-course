# Motion Graphics

_Digital Media and Creative Technology · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Motion Graphics focuses on animated text, shapes, diagrams, and visual elements used to communicate information. Students study composition, timing, typography, transitions, keyframes, and visual storytelling. Practical exercises create animated titles, explainers, or promotional sequences, helping learners combine graphic design and movement to make complex information more engaging and understandable.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up motion graphics.
- Describe composition and how it is used.
- Describe timing and how it is used.
- Describe typography and how it is used.
- Describe transitions and how it is used.
- Describe keyframes and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Digital Media and Creative Technology combines artistic communication with digital tools used to create visual, animated, interactive, and audio content. Students learn design principles, media production, modelling, editing, and immersive technologies for education, entertainment, marketing, and digital communication.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about motion graphics. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
