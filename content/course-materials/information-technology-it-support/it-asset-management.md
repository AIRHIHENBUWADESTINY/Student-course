# IT Asset Management

_Information Technology (IT) Support · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

IT Asset Management focuses on tracking and managing an organization's technology equipment and software throughout its lifecycle. Students learn asset inventories, procurement records, licensing, assignment, maintenance schedules, and retirement procedures. Practical exercises create an asset register and evaluate equipment usage, helping organizations improve accountability, control costs, and plan replacements effectively.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up it asset management.
- Describe asset inventories and how it is used.
- Describe procurement records and how it is used.
- Describe licensing and how it is used.
- Describe assignment and how it is used.
- Describe maintenance schedules and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

IT Support focuses on helping people and organizations maintain functional, secure, and reliable computer systems. Students develop troubleshooting, hardware, software, user support, administration, and service-management skills needed to resolve technical issues and keep everyday technology operating effectively.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about it asset management. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
