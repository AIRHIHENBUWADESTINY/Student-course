# IT Help Desk

_Information Technology (IT) Support · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

IT Help Desk introduces the processes used to receive, document, prioritize, and resolve technology support requests. Students learn ticket management, issue diagnosis, customer communication, escalation, knowledge bases, and service-level expectations. Practical scenarios develop professional support skills, helping learners communicate clearly, track incidents, and deliver consistent assistance to users.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up it help desk.
- Describe ticket management and how it is used.
- Describe issue diagnosis and how it is used.
- Describe customer communication and how it is used.
- Describe escalation and how it is used.
- Describe knowledge bases and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

IT Support focuses on helping people and organizations maintain functional, secure, and reliable computer systems. Students develop troubleshooting, hardware, software, user support, administration, and service-management skills needed to resolve technical issues and keep everyday technology operating effectively.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about it help desk. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
