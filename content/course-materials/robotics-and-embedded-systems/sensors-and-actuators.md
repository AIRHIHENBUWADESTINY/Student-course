# Sensors and Actuators

_Robotics and Embedded Systems · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Sensors and Actuators is part of the Robotics and Embedded Systems area. Sensors and Actuators teaches students how electronic systems detect physical conditions and produce actions. This self-study guide takes you from the core vocabulary through to a small practical project so you can learn at your own pace.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up sensors and actuators.
- Describe temperature and how it is used.
- Describe pressure and how it is used.
- Describe light and how it is used.
- Describe motion and how it is used.
- Describe distance sensors and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Robotics and Embedded Systems combines computing, electronics, sensing, and control to create devices that interact with the physical world. Students learn microcontrollers, embedded software, automation, sensors, and intelligent control for applications in industry, healthcare, transport, and consumer technology.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about sensors and actuators. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
