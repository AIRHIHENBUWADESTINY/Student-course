# Distributed Ledger Systems

_Blockchain Technology · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Distributed Ledger Systems introduces shared digital records maintained across multiple network participants. Students explore ledger structure, transactions, cryptographic hashes, replication, validation, and differences between centralized and distributed databases. Practical exercises examine simplified ledger models, helping learners understand how shared records are updated, verified, and maintained across systems with multiple participants.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up distributed ledger systems.
- Describe ledger structure and how it is used.
- Describe transactions and how it is used.
- Describe cryptographic hashes and how it is used.
- Describe replication and how it is used.
- Describe validation and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Blockchain Technology explores distributed systems that maintain shared records across participating computers. Students learn blockchain architecture, transaction validation, consensus, smart contracts, and decentralized applications, while examining security, transparency, scalability, privacy, and practical limitations of blockchain-based solutions.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about distributed ledger systems. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
