# Theory of Computation

_Computer Science · Faculty of Technology · Level 100L_

## Overview

Theory of Computation is part of the Computer Science area. Theory of Computation explores the mathematical foundations of computing. This self-study guide takes you from the core vocabulary through to a small practical project so you can learn at your own pace.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course you should be able to:

- Explain the main ideas that make up theory of computation.
- Describe mathematical foundations of computing and how it is used.
- Describe formal languages and how it is used.
- Describe finite automata and how it is used.
- Describe regular expressions and how it is used.
- Describe grammars and how it is used.
- Apply the concepts to a small, realistic task.
- Recognise common mistakes and know how to avoid them.

## Introduction

Computer Science studies the fundamental principles behind computing systems, algorithms, programming, and information processing. Students learn how computers solve problems, represent data, execute instructions, and support intelligent applications, while developing analytical thinking and computational problem-solving skills.

In this subject, the goal is not just to memorise facts but to build a reliable way of thinking about theory of computation. Work through each lesson in order, try the examples, and answer the review questions before moving on. If a term is new, write a one-line definition in your own words — that habit alone will make the material stick.
